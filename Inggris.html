01 / EXPLANATION

How Electronic Waste Is Transformed into a Decorative Lamp

01. General Statement

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the increasing use technology. Old computer keyboards and mouse devices are often thrown away because they are damaged or no longer used. However, these electronic components can still be reused to create useful products. One creative way to recycle electronic waste is by transforming old keyboard keys and an mouse into a decorative lamp called KeyLight.

02. The Transformation Process

The process begins by preparing an old keyboard, an unused mouse, and other materials needed to make the lamp, such as a light bulb, a jar, a switch, dacron, Doraemon decorations, a jar base, and a hot glue gun. First, the keyboard keys are removed and separated from the main structure. Then, the keys are cleaned to remove dust and dirt. The unused mouse is also prepared as part of the lamp decoration. After that, all materials are arranged according to the lamp design.

03. The Formation of a New Product

Next, the cleaned keyboard keys are attached to the lamp as decorative elements using a hot glue gun. The unused mouse is also added as part of the decoration. The jar is used as the main part of the lamp, while dacron and Doraemon decorations are added to make it more attractive. The switch and light bulb are installed as part of the lighting system. Finally, all materials are assembled carefully to create a decorative lamp.

04. The Result and Its Impact

As a result, old computer components are transformed into a decorative lamp called KeyLight. This product can be used as a room decoration and a source of light. Besides having aesthetic and functional value, KeyLight also helps reduce electronic waste through the 3R principle: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. Therefore, turning electronic waste into creative products is a useful way to protect the environment.

how Old Keyboards and Mouse Are Transformed into a Decorative Lamp (KeyLight)

General

Electronic Waste, such as broken or unused computer accessories, has become a growing environmental issue due to the rapid adancement of technology. Computer keyboarts and mouse are often thrown away once they stop woeking, contributing to harmful land pollution. Howefer, many of their components can still be reused creatively. One innovative way to manage this electronic waste is by transforming old keyboard keys and a mouse into a functional decorative lamp called KeyLight. This transformation demonstrates how discarded electronic items can be repurposed into valuabe, useful, and aesthetic products.

The Transformation Process

The process begins by collecting and disassembling useused keyboarts and computer mice. The plastic keycaps are carefully removed from the main keyboard structure and separated from internal circuit boards. Similary, the mouse casing is disassembled to adapt its internal click mechansim into a light swicth. Next, all the plastic keycaps and outer parts are thoroughly cleaned to remove accumulated dust, oil, and drit. At this stage, the original function of the keycaps changes completely; instead of inputting text into a computer, this are prepared to serve as light-diffusing decorative materials.

The Formation of a New Product

The cleaned keyboard keys are then attached around the outer frame or shade of a desk lamp using a durable adhesive. They are arranged in patterns-combining letters, numbers, and symbols-to create a modern, tech-themed appearance. Inside the structure, a low-voltage LED bulb and electrical wiring are installed. The switch circuit is connected to the repurposed computer mouse, allowing users to turn the lamp on and off by clicking the mouse button. Once the adhesive cures and the wiring is tested for safety, the individual components merge into a single, fully functional product.

The Result and Its Impact

As a result, discarded electronic parts are successfully transformed into KeyLight, an attractive desk lamp that blends craftsmanship with technology. The product serves a dual purpose: providing light for studying or working while adding a unique decorative touch to the room. More importantly, this transformation supports the 3R principles (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle) by reducing electronic waste and encouraging eco-friendly innovations. Repurposing computer components into valuable home decor is a practical step toward supporting environmental sustainability.

04 / EXPLANATION

How To Create A Keylight

General

Electronic waste has become a serious environmental problem caused by the rapid replacement of technological devices. Unused keyboards and computer mouse are often discarded when they are broken or no longer needed. However, these devices still contain parts that can be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by recycling old keyboard keys and an unused mouse into a decorative lamp named KeyLight. This transformation shows how electronic waste can be turned into a useful and valuable product.

The Transformation Process

The transformation begins when the unused keyboard and mouse are separated into individual components. The plastic keycaps are removed from the main keyboard structure and the switch mechanism is taken from the mouse casing. The keys are then cleaned thoroughly to remove dust and dirt. At this stage, the original function of the keyboard keys and the mouse changes. Instead of being unused for typing and controlling the cursor, they become decorative and functional materials for a new product.

The Formation of a New Product

The cleaned keys are combined with a plastic tube, a lamp base, and a small LED light to create a new object. Through the process of arranging and gluing, the individual keys form a decorative layer around the plastic tube. Inside the tube, a 5V LED strip is installed as the light source, while the modified mouse is connected with an electronic switch module to function as the lamp switch. Once all materials are firmly attached and the cables are connected, the separate components become one complete lamp.

The Result and Its Impact

As a result, an old computer keyboard and mouse are transformed into a KeyLight lamp that has both functional and aesthetic value. The product can be unused as a bedside lamp while also serving as a creative decoration. When the mouse button is clicked, the circuit connects the power to the LED so the lamp turns on, and when released, it turns off. This transformation reduces the amount of electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling computer components into a decorative lamp is one way to support a more sustainable environment and to create economic opportunity.
